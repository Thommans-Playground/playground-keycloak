# NNNN Title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decided by: the agent | the owner | together

## Context
What forces the decision. Two or three sentences.

## Decision
What we do, in the present tense.

## Alternatives considered
What else was on the table and why it was not chosen, one line each. A later reader (or reviewer) cannot trust a decision whose rejected options are not written down.

## Files that change
The files or folders this decision will touch when it is built, so a reader can check the plan before anyone builds. Write "none, a rule only" if nothing is built.

## Consequences
What gets easier, what gets harder, what we must do next. Name the cost.

## Links
Use cases, criteria, rules, owner decisions (decisions.md), other ADRs.
